Corrections

  • Apple's on-device model turns ordinary text down now and again, and when it did, Typoless corrected nothing and said nothing about it. It now asks a second time in a wording the model accepts, which gets the correction you expected.
  • Where even the second attempt is turned down, the text is left alone and the history says so, rather than reporting that there was nothing to fix.

History

  • Every pass now has a reason for each piece of text it left alone, including the ones the model would not read.
  • A line the model returned exactly as you wrote it is no longer reported as a translation.
