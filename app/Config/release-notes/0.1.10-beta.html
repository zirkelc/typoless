Corrections

  • Apple's model sometimes answers in capitals, and the whole correction used to be thrown away with them. The capitals now come off and the corrections underneath them are kept, so "teh mord is wrnog" becomes "the mord is wrong". Words you wrote yourself keep your own capitals, and a reply that holds nothing but capitals is still refused.
  • A shouted reply that mangles a word is still refused whole. Every word it changed has to be a real word in your language before any of it is used.

Saying why

  • When nothing is corrected, Typoless says why instead of "Nothing to fix", which was a claim about your writing. The reason is the same sentence the History window shows, and where there is more than one reason it points you there.
