Setting up

  • Setup is rebuilt. Six steps, one at a time, and what Typoless does now comes before what it needs.
  • Accessibility cannot be granted from inside the app that wants it, so Typoless now follows you into System Settings. While that window is in front and the permission is still missing, a small panel sits along the bottom of it with Typoless in it, ready to drag into the list. The copy you drop is the copy that gets the switch, which matters on a Mac that holds more than one.
  • The Allow button opens the Accessibility list itself. It used to stop at Privacy and Security, one level above it, with twenty categories and no reason to think Accessibility was the one.
  • The setup tour can be opened again at any time, from the new About page.

Your own words

  • A tool, a product, a surname: the Mac has never heard of any of them, and each is a few letters from a word it has heard of, so a correction can turn "pnpm" into "ppm". Settings now has Custom words, which lists the words you write that no dictionary knows, once you have written one five times. Never Change teaches the word to your Mac, for every app. Ignore drops the question.
  • The counting happens in memory only and is never written to disk.

About

  • The Privacy tab is now About, with the version, a check for updates, the links, the open-source licences and the setup tour. The sentence about password fields moved to the Apps page, next to the lists it belongs with.

Wording

  • Two messages about fields Typoless leaves alone were long enough to push the rest of the menu off the top of the screen. Both are one line now.
  • Wording across the app and the website says who does what. A refusal that said "The pass ran out of time" now says "The correction ran out of time".
