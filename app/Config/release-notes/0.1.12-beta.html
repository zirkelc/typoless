Languages

  • A line with typos is no longer taken for another language. "create teh draft, here is teh enw image" looked like Dutch, so Typoless left it alone and said it was not in a language you correct. Typos change exactly the letters that tell one language from another.
  • Before Typoless leaves a line alone because of its language, it now checks how many of the words each dictionary on your Mac knows. If more of them belong to one of your languages, Typoless corrects the line in that language. A line that is really in another language is still left alone.
