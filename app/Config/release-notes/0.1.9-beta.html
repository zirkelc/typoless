Corrections

  • A space typed in the wrong place between two words with typos in them is corrected now. "shoudlw e chnage the" becomes "should we change the". Before this, Typoless corrected one word, refused the other, and left the line worse than it found it.
  • A misspelling one letter shorter or longer than its fix is corrected. "oncistently" becomes "consistently".
  • Where the words around a moved space cannot all be corrected, none of them are, and the history says why. Your text is never left with a letter missing.
